Why you can trust it the proof

Completeness. The search space allows architectures of unbounded depth. The cutoff lemma certifies D* such that every deeper architecture is dominated by one of depth ≤ D*; a fixed-point check stops the Pareto DP once the frontier can no longer change. Every objective combination therefore has its optimum on this front.

Machine-checkable. python -m nasmo.verify certs re-derives the lemma, the layer table, each state & objective, mutual non-domination, the coverage log — and re-runs the whole search, comparing per-round frontier hashes bit-for-bit. Any tampering fails a check.

Honest scope. The guarantee is relative to the declared search space and the hardware/accuracy models; both inputs are SHA-256-pinned in the certificate.
